- [ ] Page showcasing multi-window workflow
      A page with an element. Dragging that element reveals areas in which it can be dropped. Dropping an element in that area adapts it to that area dimensions and orientation. Alternatively the element could be dragged outside of windiw. It sticks to the edge at first but after breaking the threshold turns into another window.
